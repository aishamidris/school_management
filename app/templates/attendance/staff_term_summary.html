How this is counted. A school day is any day in the term when at least one staff member checked in, so weekends and closures never count against anyone. Punctuality is on-time check-ins ÷ days present. Attendance is days present ÷ (days present + absent); approved leave days are left out of that, since being on approved leave isn't a failure to attend. Ranking goes by punctuality, then fewer total minutes late; only staff present on at least half of the school days that applied to them are ranked. Today isn't counted as an absence until the day is over.
